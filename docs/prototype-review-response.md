# Response to prototype review

Updated November 23, 2026.

| Review point | Response |
| --- | --- |
| Circle area is difficult to read precisely | Added exact annual all-food dollar labels below every dot. Labels update by year and retain their all-food meaning when a category is selected. Dot area remains a secondary cue. |
| Wide-screen space is underused | Removed the 1600 px page-width cap and reduced proportional side padding. The comparison views remain side by side on desktop; mobile stacks them. Increased both comparison chart heights to preserve label spacing. |
| Overlaid lines are clustered | Small multiples with a shared zero-based zoom are now the initial and Reset view. Users can still choose overlaid trends or the full 0–20% scale. |
| Clarify dollar basis | Summary and exact-values section now explicitly say values are not inflation adjusted. No monetary data were transformed. |
| Deploy online and improve narration | Public GitHub Pages deployment already exists and is updated with this revision. Narration has not been re-recorded. For the next recording, speak more slowly, pause after selections, and use the hosted website. |

## Verification

The prototype suite checked all 150 year/category/group selections, data tables, keyboard interaction and responsive layout. Trend tests checked all 12 category/layout/scale combinations and 150 facet selections. Focused checks verified exact dollar labels against the CSV across three representative years and all categories, and checked their bounds at 320, 390, 768, 1280, 1920 and 3440 px. Desktop and mobile screenshots were visually inspected. No new human usability sessions are claimed.

The previously submitted Week 7 ZIP and recording remain historical snapshots and were not overwritten.
