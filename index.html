READING THE DATA

A share, not an affordability score.

Each point is an average annual expenditure for a group of consumer units, not the experience of every household. The budget share is food spending divided by total spending. Income groups are ranked by income before taxes separately each year, so the lines do not track the same households. Sampling uncertainty is not shown; small differences should not be treated as statistically significant. A higher share does not by itself establish food insecurity, prices paid, or causation. The at-home and away-from-home figures may differ from total food by $1–$2 because BLS rounds published means.
