READING THE DATA

A share, not an affordability score.

Each point is an average annual expenditure for a group of consumer units, not the experience of every household. The budget share is food spending divided by total spending. A higher share does not by itself establish food insecurity, prices paid, or causation. The at-home and away-from-home figures may differ from total food by $1–$2 because BLS rounds published means.
